  1. Segment — RGB→HSV, background model from the frame border, Otsu threshold, morphological opening, largest connected component.
  2. Measure — ripe-hue fraction, blemish ratio, Sobel damage index, 8×8 uniformity cells, size fraction, circularity, aspect.
  3. Grade — weighted composite → Quality Score 0–100 → Grade A/B/C with a written explanation of the biggest deduction.
  4. Price — price_model.pkl predicts log price; the 80% band comes from the model's residual σ, widened when photo evidence is weak.
  5. Rank — every mandi scored on net value: gross − transport − commission − transit loss, payment lag shown.
  6. Decide — sell today / hold 2 days / hold 5 days, with quality decay, storage cost and falling confidence.
